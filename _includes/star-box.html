ⓘ ⁎ Conditional greatness. Of high quality that stands out clearly within its style and price bracket. A recommended buy.
⁎⁎ Absolute excellence. Exceptional quality that stands out among the greats, unconditional of age, price, or category. Probably expensive, not necessarily worth buying a bottle.
⁎⁎⁎ Transcendental. Profound quality and impact that represents a pinnacle of experience. Worth a splurge, even if only to experience it once.
