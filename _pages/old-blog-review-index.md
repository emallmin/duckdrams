<h2 id="legend">Legend</h2>

<p>My scoring system has evolved over the lifetime of the blog. Broadly speaking, I think in terms of the following categories:</p>

<figure class="wp-block-table has-small-font-size"><table><tbody><tr><td><strong><strong>❓</strong></strong></td><td><strong>Inconclusive</strong></td><td>No fair judgement can be made, for example because the sample has been tainted</td></tr><tr><td><strong><strong>❌</strong></strong></td><td><strong>Bad</strong></td><td>Poor overall quality, has significant flaws, or greatly underperforms for its category.</td></tr><tr><td></td><td><strong>Unfulfilling</strong></td><td>Not without any merit, but given the many superior alternatives there's little reason for an enthusiast to bother. This is what I expect of most commercialized single malts and blends, and single cask bottlings that did not work out too well. </td></tr><tr><td></td><td><strong>Rewarding</strong></td><td>Rewards a focussed and mindful engagement. Shortcomings, if present, are either forgivable or rather a matter of preference. This is the level I expect of official single malts with a focus on quality over marketing, and of most single casks offerings from reputable independent bottlers. <br>Spans the gamut from good to great to excellent, with different levels and combinations of deliciousness, complexity, uniqueness, flawlessness. </td></tr><tr><td><strong><strong>❤️</strong></strong></td><td><strong>Personal favourite</strong></td><td>This spirit <em>really</em> connects with me at an emotional level. The hunt for these special drams is what the hobby is all about.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure>

<p>In the index I only mark the extremes of <strong><strong>❌</strong></strong> and ❤️ since these have been consistently defined throughout the various more detailed grading schemes.</p>

<p>I also have a shorthand for some contextual factors:</p>

<figure class="wp-block-table has-small-font-size"><table><tbody><tr><td>🍷</td><td>Wine / sherry cask</td><td>Anything from sherry, to red/white wine, dessert wine, or similar, noted whenever there's more than a very light influence</td></tr><tr><td>🔥</td><td>Peated</td><td>If more than a very light influence of peat smoke</td></tr><tr><td>💎</td><td>Ultra premium</td><td>If very rare, or very expensive (€250+), or very old (30yo+ for malt, 45yo+ for grain)</td></tr><tr><td>💀</td><td>Closed distillery</td><td>No longer active, mothballed, demolished</td></tr><tr><td>◾</td><td>Tasted blindly</td><td>Review written without foreknowledge of what the dram is</td></tr></tbody></table></figure> -->
