Historically in Britain and elsewhere in Europe, a spirit that was just at the threshold of being able to be set ablaze was defined as 100 proof, although the alcohol strength to which this threshold corresponds depends a lot on temperature. Less variable, though not precisely reproducible, is the strength of spirit that gunpowder can be soaked in and still burn, working out to 57.15%. The proof can very closely be calculated as 1.75 the ABV percentage.

In celebration of the 100th post on this blog, I will try a few highlights from Signatory vintage’s 100 Proof range. SV have been putting out a lot of attractive bottlings with great price tags recently, and are for these reasons all the rage in the online whisky circles I move in.

## [Linkwood | “100 Proof” | 17yo (2006) | 57.1% | Signatory Vintage | WB259569](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/259569)
- **Info**: Bottle availablity: €80. Review: from a 5cl sample bottle
- **Nosing**: Fruity and vibrant. Sweet berries (strawberry, blueberry) and prunes, grapefruit peel, sweet saffron buns and marzipan, a mineral note and some grassiness.
- **Sipping**: Tasting neat, my first thought is how unobstrusive the alcohol is despite the high strength – dangerous! Balanced sweetness and bitterness. Flavour is tutti frutti, toffee, and a diffuse tobacco. Long-lingering dried berries and nuts in the finish.
- **Comments**: The Linkwood character is exterting itself really nicely here: I love how, even with the PX maturation, it’s not sweet and sticky, but nor is it too dry. Something I’ll consider buying.
- **Score**: Deliciousness: ⚫⚫🟢
Fun factor: ⚫🟡🟢
Value for money: ⚫⚫🟢
Punchline - Tutti frutti!

## [Aultmore | “100 Proof” | 17yo (2007) | 57.1% | Signatory Vintage | WB259568](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/259568):
- **Info**: Bottle availablity: €80. Review: from a 5cl sample bottle
- **Nosing**: Musty, with lots of buttery sherry notes and tropical fruits. Black currant, orange peel, coconut, overripe papaya; ginger, clove, nutmeg, kaffir lime; caramel, oiled leather.
- **Sipping**: Here too, the alcohol is really well integrated. Sweet, oily and round, with candied fruits, cacao butter, tobacco.
- **Comments**: I drank half of the sample bottle at first tasting, and then let the rest rest for some days and travel with me in a suitcase. The oxidization did some good work, I believe, in toning down the superficial modern sherry notes. There is a lot to discover in this dram, and I felt there was something here I could not quite put my finger on.
- **Score**: Deliciousness ⚫🟡🟢 Fun factor ⚫🟡🟢 Value for money ⚫⚫🟢
Punchline - Must-y try

## [Craigellachie | “100 Proof” | 16yo (2007) | 57.1% | Signatory Vintage | WB259567](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/259567):
- **Info**: Bottle availablity: €80. Review: from a 5cl sample bottle
- **Nosing**: Saline, green banana, hickory, net melon, sooty, flowers, toast, vanilla, custard, a little cloying
- **Sipping**: Not that sweet, a bit drying and slightly bitter in a tonic fashion. Wine gums, funky fruits, modern sherry finish.
- **Comments**: I love this distillery, and this is a good but not a standout bottling.
- **Score**: Deliciousness ⚫🟡🟢 Fun factor ⚫🟡🟢 Value for money ⚫🟡🟢
