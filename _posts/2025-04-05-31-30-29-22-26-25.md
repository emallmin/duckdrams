It’s gearing up for… MY 300TH REVIEW ON THIS BLOG! A special retrospective & statistics-so-far post is coming up to celebrate, but I also wanted to make the 300th review a special dram. In the end, I couldn’t really decide, so I’ll let fate decide. I have held onto a special “Moin 2025” tasting pack from Simple Sample since the beginning of the year. All I know is that the drams are 100% Scotch malt (although not necessarily single), and the ages represented are 22, 25, 26, 29, 30, and 31yo (not to order). What shall we find? I’m sure something will be worthy of the occasion.
