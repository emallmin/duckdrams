From the still in the 70s (1974 or 75), to the bottle in the 90s (around 1993), to my glass in 2025: these whiskies have been on a journey through time. We have here three more or less peated whiskies of almost identical vintage and age, a Springbank, an Ardbeg, and a Caol Ila. Should be an even fight!

## Springbank | 18yo (1975) | 43% | Signatory Vintage | [WB16998](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/16998)
- **Info**: Specs: Scotch single malt. Bottle availablity: €400 at recent auction. Review: from a 2cl sample bottle.
- **Nosing**: Golden fruits (ripe mango, peach, orange), green apples, white wine or perhaps mead, various desserty notes (toffee, banana bread, vanilla), beeswax, hemp, hay, farmyard funk, something burnt.
- **Sipping**: Quite grassy, peppery, earthy at first sip, then gherkin, golden sultanas, crystallized ginger, smoked cold cuts. Briney, and on the dry side.
- **Comments**: Unmistakably Springbank (says I, while acknowledging that I have been known to produce both false positives and negatives in tasting Springbank blindly)
- **Score**: 
Deliciousness   ⚫🟡🟢  
Fun factor      ⚫⚫🟢  
Value for money ⚫⚫⚫  
Punchline       70s gherkin


## Ardbeg | 19yo (1974) | 43% | Signatory Vintage | [WB7386](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/7386)
- **Info**: Specs: Scotch single malt. Bottle availablity: €600 auction. Review: from a 2cl sample bottle.
- **Nosing**: Very lemony (lemon sherbet) along with a peat and woodsmoke, white pepper, unripe nectarine, a little iodine.
- **Sipping**: Juicy, with lemon cake, pecans, peat, cigar smoke, even something flowery.
- **Comments**: Not so complicated, but really quite nice.
- **Score**: 
Deliciousness   ⚫🟡🟢    
Fun factor      ⚫🟡⚫ 
Value for money ⚫⚫⚫  
Punchline: 70s lemons


## Caol Ila | 19yo (1974) | 46% | First Cask | [WB111475](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/111475)
- **Info**: Specs: Scotch single malt. Bottle availablity: €600+. Review: from a 2cl sample bottle.
- **Nosing**: Various yellow fruits, limoncello, litchi, “Champis”, sponge cake, fresh grass, cinnamon, oak spice
- **Sipping**: Salty, just a little sweet and peppery. More peat than on the nose, turning a bit vegetal, plenty of citrus, salted grapefruit, and definitely oatcakes.
- **Comments**: Another pretty good one! Not super-memoranle
- **Score**: 
Deliciousness   ⚫🟡🟢  
Fun factor      ⚫🟡⚫  
Value for money ⚫⚫⚫   
Punchline: Peated oatcakes


As anticipated, quite an even battle, and I can’t pick a clear winner, or loser even!
