<em><a href="https://www.thewhiskyjury.com/">The Whisky Jury</a></em> is the Belgian independent bottling company of a guy called Joeri (the pun is intended). Launching in 2019 with two vintage Ben Nevises and a Caol Ila bottling, they have had a <a href="https://www.whiskylifestyle.com/an-interview-with-joeri-from-the-whisky-jury/">slow but growing success</a>, with over a 100 bottlings to date (including some malternatives). According to their website <em>"We are so picky, you can open a bottle of ours with confidence. We would be ashamed to disappoint you."</em> Indeed, the whiskybase ratings are generally very high — and so are the prices, though not unreasonable in today's market conditions. I discovered this bottler at the <a href="https://duckdrams.com/2025/05/03/limburg-2025/">Limburg Whisky Festival 2025</a>, where I was explained that <em>fruitiness</em> was a guiding word in the cask selection. Right up my alley then! I tried a couple of delightful samples as the festival, and brought home with me a bunch for a calmer tasting session.
