When was I last in Copenhagen? I can barely remember. At least 15 years ago. Now I have returned to probe the prospects of taking up an academic position here (if my grant applications are successful). Of course, I must check out the whisky scene. From my online searches it seems [Liedkoeb](https://lidkoeb.dk/whisky-bar/) is the premier whisky watering hole in this town. And fancy it is. I needed to reserve a seat in their whisky attic in advance. 

The place offers a great atmosphere and a selection of ~150 whiskies. Once I've pruned their whisky manu from the non-scotch, regular OB expressions, and less interesting (for me) single casks, there are a handful of drams that I was keen to try.

## The drams

I started off with a battle of Tobermories, then a Royal Lochnagar, a Ben Nevis, and an Invergordon. In parallel I was (ultimately unsuccessfully) bidding on a bottle of Imperial at the online auctions. I scribbled down some tasting notes:


### [Tobermory 20yo 50% W&M](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/66053/tobermory-1994-wm)

N: Baked apples, oak spice (cinnamon), citrus (lemon), fruit punsch, canned tropical fruits, perfumy, musky, minerality, coffee, digestive, hint of smoke.

P: Quite strong, musty sherry. Sweet and a bit savoury. Mango chutney, creme brulee, dried mulberries, a little cigar smoke.

### [Tobermory 20yo 53.9% The Pearls of Scotland](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/98614/tobermory-1996-gc)

N: Quite similar, but crispier: apple-y, fruity, some perfume. Also some dunnage, white pepper.

P: Briney and rather dry. Even some tannins? Grassy, dry apple cider, maltiness, buscuits, some ash. Water brings out more fruits. 

### [Royal Lochnagar 16yo 57.5%](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/192753/royal-lochnagar-16-year-old)

N: Genreally a nice bourbon cask profile. Woody, floral (rosehip), sponge cake with icing.

P: Malty, greasy, "unripe". At first a weird farmy kind of burnt dung or dry grass note that that then subsided. Melon and ham.

### [Ben Nevis 9yo 61.1% Deer Stalker](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/216603/ben-nevis-2012-ds)
Lots of red fruits/berries. More like a port casks than sherry. Drinkable, good body, a little rubber.

### [Invergordon 28yo 56.8% The Pearls of Scotland](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/86078/invergordon-1988-gc)

Wood and glue. A flavour reminscent of bourbon whiskey (that's the grain). Lots of vanilla. On the palate, sweet, lots of pastry with almonds, coconut flakes.

## Verdict

The prices were not at all bad, I spent 515 DKK (~€70) on the five drams below. I would surely come again, if I had some company. And next time I might try their specialty whisky drinks.
