Vallein Tercinier cognac makes another appearance on this blog, in a truly special octoenarian form: a Grande Champagne cru from 1934, and a bons bois bru from the 1940s, both beyond 80 years in cask. Some of the most expensive samples I have bought, at €71.90 and €59.90 for 5cl each. This special moment was shared with my father, as we let ourselves be transported back in time.
