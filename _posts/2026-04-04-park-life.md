Highland Park is one of the very few distilleries I only "discovered" quite late in my whisky journey. I was always put off by their marketing, and the independent bottlers usually go under "Secret Orkney" or "Whitlaw" and did not catch my attention. But since there're only two distilleries on Orkney, and Scapa is rare as an IB, it's pretty revealing. Perhaps why HP seems to now opposed referencing Orkney, so new IBs just appear as "Secret Highland".  

My first HP review on this blog was #150 from a blind tasting, not a convincing drop, then next time #350, quite the more convincing. Enough for me finally try out a whole spectrum of the HP sauce, and somehow a few months later I've got 5 IB bottles in the stash.

Since last year I've noticed a deluge of IB HPs releasing. A new trend or just my new awareness? At least, I reckon the steady flow of high age-statement ones at quite seductive prices is a new trend. One of them is the "Park Life" (yet another pseudonym). A actually pounced on the 22yo and ordered both the bottle and a sample, together with a sample of the little brother 20yo. Time finally to see if I bet on the right HP-horse. And let's throw in a couple of other HP samples for good measure.
