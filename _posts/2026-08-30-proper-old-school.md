Some conclusion I draw, is that compared to modern whisky, these old schoolers tend to:
- Are plenty expressive and often decently mouthfilling despite only 40% or 43% ABV
- Follow a style with a subtle sherry influence with lots of depth, not a heavy overcoat
- Become splendidly waxy on the nose with time in the glass

Perhaps whisky was better back in the day? I would say: standard off-the-shelf single malts probably were. But there's a lot more variation today, and probably more consistent quality.
