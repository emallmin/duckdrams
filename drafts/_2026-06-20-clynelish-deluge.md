## 1 Compass Box
Nose: Alcohol punch. Bright, fruity, zesty. Lychee, papaya, tropical. Bit of floral honey, hint of wax, cedar wood. 
Sipping: Sweet, not hot. Clean, fruity profile, with pear, sweet grapefruit, hint of salt. Gooseberry/bourbon finish. 
Comment: Really good nose. Delicious, if not too charismatic. A very solid summer dram. In the finial revisit felt a bit thin compared to the competition. Guess: opened sample, tamed.
Score: N87 S84 T85

## 2 Adelphi
Nose: Bit shy. Grapefruit, white pepper, mineral. After a little while, denser bourbon cask notes appear,  
Sipping: Sweet, with a very lucid passion fruit note. Then salted grapefruit. Some richness and depth. Upon final return a bit more bitter than necessary. 
Comment: Lovely 
Score: N85 S86 T85

## 3 PST
Nose: Still fruity, but more pie crumble, roasted malt. And grapy, somewhat sour and woody note. Bit saline
Sipping: Flavourful, with sweetness, salinity, and some savouriness. Slight metallic note in the finish.
Comment: Should be the PST. Flavour description sound not to great, but it's better than that. Lot of character as quirk. 
Score: N83 S86 T85

## 4 James Eadie - best?
Nose: Bit strong. Juicy nectarines, baked apples, banana, some oak spice. Getting some fatty notes, and burning candle. 
Sipping: Sweet, briny, juicy. Also a bit of the metallic finish. 
Comment: Classic. Better than #3 I think, but similar direction.

## 5 Carn Mor
Nose: Cold cream, slightly buttery. Hint of thyme, some citrus. Not a fruit bomb. Possible wax note  
Sipping: Medium sweet, dense and oily, but also really quite bitter grapefruit rind, and with some tobacco-like undertones.  
Comment: Probably the Adelphi?
