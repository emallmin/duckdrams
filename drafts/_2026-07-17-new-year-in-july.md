#1 tullibaridine-malaga
nose:
Fresh, sprite-y, very citrussy, a bit grassy, apples & pears; summery. 

palate:
Sprit-driven, with a  , sweet maltiness, light grassy notes, grapefruit.  

guess:
Tasty, competent, if in no way extraordinary single malt. Something like a Fettercairn or Deanston 12? 46% Mainly bourbon casks.

84

That Malaga cask is completely invisible. Otherwise the specs check out.


#2 bunnahabhain-11-sv
nose: 
Woody, nutty, toasted, red berries. Aromatic, and a bit sweaty/musky too. 

palate:
Medium to high sweetness, lots of wood spice, cinnamon buns, pruny, leather.   

comment:
More interesting on the nose than the palate. Hesitating between Oloroso and PX, but let's go Oloroso. Highland malt. Could be an every so lightly peated HP, but more likely something else.

Ah, Bunnahabhain checks out!
85

#3 aberlour-abunadh-2025
nose:
Brown sugar, treacle, cola sauce, orange marmalade; rich sherry.

palate:
sweet, salted caramel, honey-waffle, sherried, completely cask-driven, but a sweet treat. 

guess:
sherry cask, closer to 49%, 9-13 years old, no distillery signature.

Abunadh makes perfect sense. Been a while since I had one.
85

#4 orkney-14-sv

Nose: Yet another sherry bomb. Big on he red fruits and berries, also a slight earthy dampness. Strong alcohol. 

Palate: Now theres rich but rather dry sherry playing off against a light, earthy and herbaceous peat.

Guess: I'll be damned if this is not Highland Park. 11 years old, at a decently high cask strength, sherry cask, possible a fresh finish.

85

#5 ardbeg-smokiverse
nose: beech-smoked ham, sea spray, earthy, citrus-fresh, apricot 

palate: minty, fresh, green, saline, rather ashy in the finish

guess: Why no ex-bourbon Caol Ila? No reason to be older than 8. Cask strength around 56%. 

87

#6 lingdarroch
nose: vanilla is the first note I get; a bit closed (or my nose is closing up). Light fruity/tropical notes, some chalky/minerally freshness. Now it's opening up and I get just oodles of honey. Fatty fish-n-chips newspaper. Pommegrenade juice and green apple. 

palate: heavy peat, tarred rope, juicy yellow fruits, candi sugar, rather medicinal, herbal, metholic.   

guess: again Islay, could well be a Laphi of some sort. The sweetness speaks of some sweet wine finish - maybe sauternes? 10+- age.

Unexpectedly, it's a 3-year old peated Bladnoch in an Amarone cask.
87
