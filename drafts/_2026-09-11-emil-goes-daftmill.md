

## Winter batch 2010
- **Nosing:** Fruits and pastries. Lots of vanilla framing the whole experience: ripe pears and peaches, but also some sour freshness of citrus or winter apples. Sponge cake and biscuits, etc., sprinkle of cinnamon. A bit of earthy, lightly dunnag-y smell and fresh baking yeast — a 'boutique' whisky signature, perhaps.
- **Sipping**: Full-bodied, balnced; caramel, fruit compotte, I  
- **Comment:** It is obviously very well-made whisky. It is very tasty. But maybe it's just a little boring, at the end of the day? At least for €100+
- **Score**: 86

### Summer Batch 2009
- **Nosing:** Rich sherry, of the cola-ginger-ale-toffee kind. And cheesecake, maybe? At the same time fresh and vibrant while also letting through leather and cigar-box notes and oak. 
- **Sipping:** Rather dry, but with more lingering sweetness, definitely some woody astingency and trailing bitterness. Real sherry notes, toasted nuts, coffee, oak wood, almost meaty in a beef-jerky way - that faint sulphury note that does not register as sulphur, really. The flavours perhaps fall off a bit too fast in the finish.
- **Comments:** Sherry suits this Daftmill like a tight leather glove. Possibly I'm biased by the Daftmill hype, but I do sense a kinship with old-school sherried whiskies from the 70s. Maybe just as a matter of using real quality sherry casks in concert with bourbon? 
- **Score**: 88 

### 15
- **Nosing**: Neat, it's a bit timid, and I dare say generic. Marhsmallows, vanilla, lemon candy. With time/water I get more grapefruit, pine tree and resin, developing into unripe mango and grassy-floral notes, freshly baked sourdough bread, bit of waxiness. 
- **Sipping:** More on the dry side, very malty, clean and precise, signs of maturity, perhaps some sake, but ultimately rather little complexity. That's before water. I felt water did wonders to the nose, but by that time the palate is thinning out. 
- **Comment:** The water made a 2-point difference on the nose, for sure. Perhaps I'd enjoy it more if I could find that precise water-balance, but on first try I have to admit I was expecting more.
- **Score:** 85 

### MoM
- **Nosing**: Quite aromatic neat: garden flowers, pumpkin (?), atop that very clean and fresh smells of grapefruit, whip-cream, fresh baking yeast, white pepper (sneeze!). Water again bring light waxiness, amplifies the vanillas. 
- **Sipping**: Huh, feels more "lower register" than the nose implies; very robust, malty, spirit spirit-driven and even meaty and subtly fuming like a Craigellachie; a little rough and wild in a totally likeable way. Textured through restrained sweetness and some mild bitterness. Flavourwise there's white dessert-wine quality (like a Sauternes), but without the sugar. With water, more tropical fruit, rounder and creamier; premium milk chocolate, and surprisingly aged tobacco-y notes.
- **Comment:** Here is finally that zest for life, that expressive personality! 
- **Score**: 87
