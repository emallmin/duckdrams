# light
- **Info**:
- **Nosing**: lots of tropical fruits: kiwi, ripe mango, peaches... joined by vanilla, milk chocolate, caramel, and a touch of hay and grass. With patiance some intimations of mature and rancio'd cognac notes.
- **Sipping**: Creamy, fruity amd jammy: peaches, sweet clementines, candy banana. And quite a bit sour, with character of apple cider vinegar. Plenty of vanilla oad oak spice.
- **Comments**: First impression, it's like cake with fruits and cream. Very delicious, generous, easy to like. Though I didn't feel like I absolutely must have this bottle, the €160 is actually quite reasonable. 
- S: 88

# dark
- **Info**:
- **Nosing**: One of those noses with clear sulphur, but it's not necessarily an off note. Toasted and burnt notes, like deep roasted coffee, dark caramel, mix with rubber, leather, and vinuous, balsamico-like notes, pruciuitto and dried fruits and nuts. Luckily the nose rather restrained so these heavier notes do not become overwhelming.
- **Sipping**: Quite assertive, with present alcohol, salty sweetness, and rich flavours. Pulls more on the sweet fruits, prunes, sultanas, chocolate orange, and the like, but also cured meats and the odd pickle. Some astringency and lots of oak spice. The sulphury aspect shows much less on the palate (and it's for the best).
- **Comments**: This is an interesting and tasty Irish, it's a bit inelegant but quite fun. 
-S: 87
