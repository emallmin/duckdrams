## Aurora
- N: Pleasant mix of fresh bourbon and sherry cask influences, with a hint of mineral and smoke. Crisp apples and pears, some strudel with vanilla sauce, abundant spice. With patience, a certain oily, perhaps waxy character comes forth. 
- P: Concordant with with the nose, it's fresh and mildly sweet, with orchard fruits, cherries, perhaps some brownies, and an oaky backbone. Tastes somewhat young, like unrestrained in the vanilla and spice, but not harsh.
- C: Apparently 50/50 sherry and bourbon, 5% peated malt. Youngest component is just shy of 6 years, with an average of 7. Overall just a nice drinking whisky, whithout bells or whistles, perhaps a bit 
- S: 83

## Älv
- I: 100% and 100% non-peated Swedish pilsner malt.
- N: More focussed, crisply fruity, than Aurora. Pineapple, coconut, grapefruit, candy banana. By now, really quite waxy (in a coconut way), almost a hint of sweat, and faint   
- P: Honest and quite focussed, neither sweet nor dry, full-bodied, maltied, and bringing fruity, candy-like aromas , and a slighly phenolic touch.
- C: Lars says this is the hardest kind of whisky to make: young, unpeated, bourbon cask. Certainly, there's nothing for flaws in the new make to hide behind. And here there are none. Bravo!
- S: 86

## Hav 25% peated
- N: Dulce de leche, toasted coconut, espresso, hint of tomato vine
- P: Salted caramel, spicy and armatic oak, vanilla, "keks", and a decent helping of peat.

## Berg
- N. A quite complex, winey but mellow nose. Perhaps some cola, and toasted nuts, ripe pineapple, dried fruits. I get some association to my Le Nez du Whisky: a perfumy, indescribable mix of all the nosing vials in there. 
- P: Balanced taste, sweet associations but not sugary. Concentrated fruit flavours, strawberry coulis, dark chocolate. 
- C: I really like Lars' preference for using also bourbon casks in sherry expression to balance the sweetness.
- S: 85

The most transparent distillery in the world.

## Timmer
Belgium and Scotland malt. 100% around 7yo
- N: Fresh, coastal, Islay style. Citrus, philadelphia with herbs.
- P: Pleasant sweetness, malty, brightly fruity, with bergamot, and a rather earthy and -. Somewhat rawly peaty in the aftertaste.  
- C: Blind I would have bet all my money on a youngish bourbon-cask Islay. Ironically, it's highland peat.
- S: 85
