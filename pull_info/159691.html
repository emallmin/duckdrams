    • Nose
      89 89
      Fresh, sweet, and brightly fruity – like a basket of apricots, pears, and citrus fruits next to a warm oak crate. The Bourbon contributes vanilla and honey, while the Oloroso provides nuttiness, raisins, and a subtle dryness. Overall, bright, clear, and pleasantly complex.
    • Taste
      81 81
      Round, sweet and spicy, and vibrant – like a golden thread of honey trickling over bright fruits, caramel, and warm oak. Bourbon provides vanilla and creaminess, while Oloroso adds structure, nuttiness, and a slightly dry undertone. The texture is clean, modern, and well-balanced without becoming heavy.
    • Finish
      85 85
      Medium-long, fruity-woody and pleasantly warm. Vanilla, raisin, light fruits and a subtle hint of spice linger – like the final notes of a mild Andalusian evening.
