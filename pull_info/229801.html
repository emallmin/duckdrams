  1. Rittergold scored this whisky 85 points
    04 may 2025 5:40 pm

    The nose is a bit simple with tobacco and leather notes. The palate is a raspy growl of fiery smoke and ginger spice, all with a brown sugar. If I had to hazard a guess, this might be Lagavulin.

    Nose - leathery peat, tobacco, with a hint of oysters and lemon

    Taste medium body, sooty fiery smoke, light brown sugar, spicy ginger chews, peach tones hiding in the back, citrus,

    Finish - mesquite smoke, limeade, slight iodine
