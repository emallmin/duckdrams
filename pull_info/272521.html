  1. Stefan_Hartl scored this whisky 85 points
    16 jan 2026 6:28 pm

    This Glengoyne is characterized by its lovely fruitiness and spice. The sherry cask influence is barely noticeable.
    • Nose
      On the nose, the malt is immediately very fruity, sweet, and fresh, making it very pleasant. Yellow apples and peaches are dominant, but I also detect apricots and berries. The alcohol is well-integrated, with only a hint of freshness in the background. There are also some honey and caramel notes. I can't detect any sherry cask influence, but the fruitiness makes for a lovely nose nonetheless.
    • Taste
      The whisky glides smoothly over the tongue, offering predominantly apple and peach notes. A peppery spice provides a satisfying start. On the palate, the whisky can also be described as fresh, fruity, sweet, and spicy. Later, a hint of orange emerges.
    • Finish
      Fruity and spicy, with a distinctly dominant apple note in the finish. The oak becomes more pronounced, the mouthfeel quickly becomes drier, and spices emerge. Dry spices, oak, and apple notes linger on the palate for a medium length of time.
