  1. Stefan_Hartl scored this whisky 84 points
    17 jan 2026 5:04 pm

    A fairly simple whisky for its age, yet delicious, with tropical fruit notes and noticeable oak spice. The grain content seems quite high.
    • Nose
      The nose opens with a sugary, popcorn-like sweetness and tropical fruits. I primarily detect pineapple, apricots, peaches, and a hint of banana. Alongside the sweet notes, a subtle oaky spice is also discernible, and despite the relatively low alcohol content, there's a slight tingle from the alcohol on the nose. Overall, the sweet, tropical fruits predominate. After some time in the glass, I also detect a touch of vanilla and honey.
    • Taste
      The whiskey glides smoothly and very mildly over the tongue, surprisingly offering only a hint of sweetness. This sweetness is reminiscent of cotton candy, leading me to suspect a fairly high grain content. Then a slightly bitter oak spice emerges, somewhat reminiscent of green tea. The fruit notes remain exotic; I find it difficult to pinpoint them precisely, but I suspect peaches and pineapple, perhaps also gooseberries.
    • Finish
      The finish starts sweet and sugary, again with strong associations of cotton candy and pineapple. In contrast, the bitter notes of green tea reappear. The emerging oak spice quickly makes the finish drier. Dry and sugary flavors linger on the palate for a short to medium length of time.
